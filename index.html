a little about me

Hi, I'm Vedika, a first-year at UCLA studying Design Media Arts, which is a very official-sounding way of saying I like making things and refuse to pick just one medium.

My family splits pretty evenly into two camps: engineers (my dad, both my older brothers) and artists (my mom, who's actually an art teacher, and my grandma, who can sew, paint, or cook almost anything into existence). I spent a while trying to pick a side, coding classes, data science, straight-up art classes, an interior design course, before realizing I didn't have to choose.

More to come as the year goes on. ♡
